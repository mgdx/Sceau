Sceau reads the chip of ICAO 9303 identity documents over NFC and checks that they are genuine. It works entirely offline: no network permission, nothing is kept, nothing is sent.

<b>Supported documents</b>
<ul>
<li>French national identity card (credit card format, since 2021), using the 6-digit CAN printed on the card</li>
<li>European identity cards, using the CAN or the MRZ</li>
<li>Biometric passports from any country, using the document number, date of birth and expiry date</li>
</ul>
To avoid typing mistakes, the MRZ (the band of text at the bottom of the passport page or on the back of the card) can be read with the camera: the document number and dates are filled in, and you check them before starting the reading. Recognition runs inside the app, with no external service.

<b>What Sceau shows</b>
<ul>
<li>The photo stored in the chip</li>
<li>Identity: surname, given names, sex, date of birth, nationality, document number, issuing state, expiry date</li>
<li>Additional data when the chip holds it (place of birth, address, issuing authority and date of issue…)</li>
</ul>

<b>What Sceau verifies</b>
<ul>
<li>The issuing state's signature over the data, up to a known root certificate, and the integrity of each data group (Passive Authentication)</li>
<li>That the chip is not a clone, when the document supports it (Chip Authentication, Active Authentication)</li>
</ul>
The result is a clear verdict (Genuine, Valid signature but chip not verified, Unknown issuer, Failed) with a detailed checklist.

<b>Trust store</b>
Sceau ships the French CSCA certificates published by ANTS and the German Master List published by BSI, which covers the European Union, the EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom among others. For a document whose issuer is not included, the verdict is "Unknown issuer"; you can import a Master List published by another state. Revocation lists are not checked. Sceau is not affiliated with any of these organisations.

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>Permissions: NFC, and camera only to read the MRZ. No photo is taken or saved: images are analysed in memory then wiped, and the name is never read. The camera is optional: you can always type the fields</li>
<li>No data read from the chip is saved; memory is wiped when leaving the result or when the app goes to the background</li>
<li>Screenshots are blocked throughout the app</li>
<li>No ads, no trackers, no Google services</li>
</ul>

<b>Intended use</b>
Sceau is meant for checking your own document, or a document presented by its holder. Under the GDPR, the photo is biometric data when used for automated comparison: Sceau does not perform such comparison.

Sceau does not read fingerprints or iris data.
