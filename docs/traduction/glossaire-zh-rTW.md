# Glossary zh-rTW (Traditional Chinese, Taiwan)

Taiwanese vocabulary (not a character conversion from Simplified Chinese): 晶片, 憑證, 資料, 檔案, 設定, 應用程式, 匯入, 簽章. Informal 你, as in Android in Taiwan. Space between Chinese characters and Latin words or digits. Full-width punctuation (，。：（）「」）. ICAO acronyms and “Master List” not translated.
